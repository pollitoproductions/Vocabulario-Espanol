Privacy Policy — Classroom Site

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

This policy covers the classroom version of the Pollito Productions website at pollitoproductions.github.io/Vocabulario-Espanol/, which Mr. Fuller built for his Spanish classes. It is written for parents and guardians. This classroom site has no ads.

1. What this site collects

This is a free learning site. Students do not create an account, and the site does not ask for a name, email address, or any other personal information.

  • Game progress: Some games and tools save a student's progress (such as high scores or a saved game) in the student's own web browser, using local storage. That information stays on the student's device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. It can be cleared at any time by clearing the browser data.
  • Chatbot messages: The Pollito chatbot lets students practice Spanish (or English) by chatting with an AI tutor. When a student types a message, it is sent to Groq, the AI service that powers the chatbot, only to generate a reply. Groq does not use student messages to train its models. Mr. Fuller also keeps a private log of chatbot conversations (the messages and replies, with the date and time) in a Google Sheet that only he can see, so he can check that students are safe and using the chatbot well. The chatbot never asks for a student's name, address, phone number, school, or any other personal information. Students are told not to share anything sensitive in their messages.
  • Hosting: This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home or school addresses, or any other personal information from visitors to this site. We do not sell any information, and we do not share information with advertisers. There is no advertising on this classroom site.

3. Third-party services used by this site

  • Groq (chatbot): Processes chatbot messages to generate replies. Groq does not use student messages to train its models. See Groq's privacy policy for how it handles that data.
  • GitHub Pages (hosting): Hosts this website. See GitHub's privacy statement for how it handles server logs.

4. Children's privacy

This site is used by students, including children under 13, as a free learning tool for Mr. Fuller's classes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The chatbot is designed not to ask for any personal information, including a name. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared personal information through this site, please contact us at the email address below and we will help.

5. Data deletion

Because this site does not store personal information on its servers, there is generally nothing for us to delete. A student's game progress lives only in their own browser and can be cleared there. Chatbot messages are kept only in the teacher's private log described above — if you would like a message removed from that log, please email us at:

pollitoproductions.dev@gmail.com

6. The public site and mobile apps

The public version of Pollito Productions (pollitoproductions.github.io) and our Google Play games are covered by a separate privacy policy at pollitoproductions.github.io/privacy-policy.html. This page covers the classroom site only.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will show when it last changed.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at pollitoproductions.dev@gmail.com.
