An ark bound for another star. Spin gravity holds the decks — thrust is the only other gravity you’ll get. Nine glowing docking ports: eight around the hull, one at the top end-cap hub. Fly within 100 m of any port and you dock automatically — there is no dock button. Hitting the hull or an asteroid anywhere else ends the run. An asteroid belt drifts clear of the ship — dodge the rocks; aura asteroids can be mined (hold M) for water from comets and metal from metal rocks. Jumps inside the ship: the ground slides beneath you while you’re airborne, because the ship keeps spinning and you don’t. (Jump arcs float Moon-like by movement tuning; the ship’s spin gravity itself is unchanged.) After undocking, docking re-arms once you fly 150 m clear of every port, so launching never snaps you straight back aboard. Inside, the ship is divided into sections: AGRICULTURE aft (open farmland, Greenhouse cell) and the enclosed industrial half forward, split into TECHNOLOGY (Workshop cell, plus a two-story gallery hall with stair towers and a railed upper gallery) and EDUCATION (Classroom cell); a bulkhead with four glowing entrances divides the halves, and a MEDICAL BAY room (Dictation). Walk into a glowing GREENHOUSE, WORKSHOP, CLASSROOM, or MEDICAL BAY doorway to step into its cell, and leave by the EXIT door, the EXIT button, or E. Beacon range: a warning appears past 20 km from the ship; past 30 km you are LOST and the run ends (retry returns you aboard, haul kept). A cyan beacon beam rises from the nearest port while you fly, and the nav readout adds an up/down elevation angle, so guidance works in 3D — but it only ever points, it never steers or returns you. Farther out, the belt stretches to about 20 km, and all 1,000 Spanish vocabulary words float among the rocks — fly within 250 m of one to pause and pronounce it; say it right to collect it for +5 WATER and +5 METAL. A gold word arrow under the port readout points to the nearest uncollected word, and distant words show as small glow dots until their plates stream in. Crash repairs cost 10 WATER + 10 METAL; your first crash is free. If you can't pay, the journey ends.

GENERATION SHIP — ARK-7

The ARK-7 carries more than people to the stars — it carries a second language. Back home, Spanish is fading from daily life. Aboard this ship, it lives: in the words drifting among the asteroids, in the classrooms and workshops, in the voices of the crew. Your journey: collect every word, speak it aloud, and keep the language alive for the next generation.
