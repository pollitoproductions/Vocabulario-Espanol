Student worksheets

Print-and-go practice pages to use alongside the games.

Each worksheet sends you back into the game to find and collect words, then helps you practice them on paper. Topics include the el and la gender rules, numbers and math, countries, colors, animals, verbs, jobs, food, weather and nature, directions, and building simple sentences. There is also a scavenger hunt, a short quiz, and a reflection page for the end of a lesson.
